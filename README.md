I'm a physics graduate (BS Physics, BZU Multan, CGPA 3.61) who works across the whole stack of modern computing. I've modelled MOSFETs from the semiconductor equations, designed oscillators at transistor level, taken an Edge-AI accelerator from RTL to signoff GDSII on SkyWater SKY130, and shipped full-stack apps and LLM agents in production. I'm most interested in **low-power IC design, analog/mixed-signal circuits, and AI hardware**.
